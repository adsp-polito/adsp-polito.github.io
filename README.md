The course is offered in English in the first semester of the 2nd year of Data Science and Engineering at Politecnico di Torino.

Learn to turn ideas into innovative AI applications for digital and physical environments. Start with people’s needs, explore what emerging AI models make possible, and design, develop and validate a solution through a team project. Human-centred design, stakeholder mapping, user personas and functional requirements connect technological opportunities with the people who will use the solution.

The project mindset has four foundations: Design, Develop, Communicate and Manage. Design frames user needs and requirements; Develop brings together data, models, implementation and validation; Communicate explains the results through technical and scientific communication. Manage supports all three through innovation management, planning, coordination, key performance indicators (KPIs) and risk management.

The course combines 30 hours of lectures with 50 hours of laboratory activities. Explore language, vision and multimodal models, retrieval-augmented generation, transfer learning, AI ethics and the Sustainable Development Goals, alongside an introduction to vision-language-action models and embodied AI. Apply these concepts through practical case studies, team project development and presentation checkpoints.
